      Finding your TVs
      
      
      
        The TV must be on and on the same Wi‑Fi as this computer (not a guest network). On macOS, allow the app under Privacy & Security → Local Network. Routers with client isolation block discovery; the network scan still finds TVs by probing their ports. Last resort: add the TV by IP address.
